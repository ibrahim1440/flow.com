SECTION 1 — PURPOSE OF THIS FILE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ Purpose of This File

This file defines the **Accounting Module** of the Hiqbah ERP and how it fits with the rest of the platform (inventory, production, roasting, QC, sales, purchasing, café/POS, branches, integrations). It is the financial backbone of the system.

The file's stated goals are:

- Move from a model where an external accounting system (Qoyod / Qyood) is the source of truth to a model where the ERP itself generates accounting events internally, and uses Qoyod only as a transitional export/sync target.
- Define accounting policies that the rest of the ERP must respect (inventory costing, COGS recognition, waste treatment, VAT, customer advances, payment terms, branch accounting).
- Specify the chart of accounts, journal entry rules, invoice rules, payment rules, approval rules, fiscal period rules, and reporting outputs.
- Capture the policy decisions made after a pre-development Q&A round, so developers have a single normative reference.

The file is **not a UI specification** and **not a code-generation contract**. It is the operational and data-logic contract that the Accounting, Inventory, Production, Sales, Purchasing, and Integration services must obey.

It is also the file that decides whether the ERP can ever become its own accounting system or remains a satellite tool around Qoyod. The chosen direction is **gradual replacement of Qoyod**, with the ERP being the long-term system of record. Every design choice below is evaluated against that direction.

SECTION 2 — CLEAN TECHNICAL SUMMARY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ Clean Technical Summary

The Accounting Module is a **policy-driven, event-sourced financial layer** that:

- Receives **Accounting Events** from every operational module (inventory movement, production, roasting, QC, transformation, packaging, sales, purchasing, payments, waste, calibration, branch transfer, period closing).
- Translates each event into one or more **Draft Journal Entries** based on per-tenant accounting policies (costing method, COGS policy, waste treatment, VAT category, branch-accounting mode).
- Routes drafts through an **approval workflow** when required, posts them to the **General Ledger**, and emits an **export job** for Qoyod during the transition period.
- Maintains the **Chart of Accounts**, **Fiscal Periods**, **Tax Categories**, **Customer/Supplier Accounts**, **Customer Advances**, **AR/AP aging**, **Inventory Valuation**, **COGS**, **Waste accounts**, and **Reports**.
- Enforces hard rules: no silent edits after posting, no posting in closed periods, no posting on parent accounts, no reuse of customer advances, no transformation treated as new production, no production overhead allocation in Phase 1.
- Is **tenant-scoped end to end**: chart of accounts, settings, mappings, journal entries, invoices, payments, fiscal calendar, reports — every record carries `tenant_id` and every query filters by it.
- Supports the **multi-phase rollout** declared in the source: Phase 1 (basic accounting + CSV export to Qoyod), Phase 2 (inventory, production, waste, COGS, POS, API export), Phase 3 (ZATCA-grade official invoicing from ERP, multi-currency, full SaaS tenant accounting).

The module's invariant is captured at the end of the source file and is the **single most important rule** for the developer:

> No financial or inventory movement is final until it has produced an Accounting Event. No silent edit after posting or export. Correction is always Reversal / Adjustment / Credit Note / Debit Note.

This invariant must be enforced **server-side**, not by UI conventions.

SECTION 3 — ORIGINAL BUSINESS INTENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ Original Business Intent

The author's intent is a sequenced, defensible accounting strategy:

1. The ERP must own the accounting logic internally from day one — even if the official invoice is still emitted by Qoyod during the transition. The ERP must not be reduced to a "feeder" that pushes raw data to Qoyod and trusts Qoyod's interpretation.
2. The ERP must support **practical defaults for a small roastery / café** (Weighted Average costing, COGS on shipping for B2B, COGS on sale for POS, Cost-Center-only branch accounting, alert-only credit policy, SAR currency, 15% VAT default) while leaving the door open for richer setups (FIFO, branch accounting, multi-currency, international suppliers, advanced overhead allocation) without re-architecting later.
3. Waste must not hide inside generic COGS. **Calibration Waste**, **QC Testing Waste**, **Abnormal Roasting Loss**, and **Training Waste** are first-class accounts.
4. Customer money received in advance is a **liability**, not revenue. Customer advances are tracked through their own lifecycle (Requested → Received → Applied → Closed / Refunded).
5. **Blending and product transformation are not production** for accounting purposes — they consume source lots and create a new lot at the same cost, with no revenue and no COGS recognition.
6. The transition with Qoyod must be **safe** (idempotent export keys, mapping tables, reconciliation reports, no silent edits, retry tracking, payload/response snapshots) so that the eventual departure from Qoyod does not corrupt historical data.
7. The architecture must remain **SaaS-ready**: per-tenant settings, per-tenant chart of accounts, per-tenant tax categories, per-tenant fiscal calendar, per-tenant mappings — all enforced.
8. ZATCA readiness is built in from the structure (UUID, QR, XML hash, invoice type, exemption reason) even though Phase 1 doesn't generate the official e-invoice from ERP.

This intent is sound. The rewrite below preserves it, sharpens the boundaries between operational and accounting events, and flags risks where the source is permissive or under-specified.

SECTION 4 — BUSINESS LOGIC
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━1 Master invariants (binding across the module)

1. Every operational event that affects money or inventory **MUST** generate an Accounting Event server-side before the operation is considered complete.
2. Accounting Events produce **Draft Journal Entries**; postings happen only after validation (and approval where required).
3. **Debit = Credit** is enforced at the journal entry level; partial posting is rejected.
4. **Parent accounts** in the chart of accounts cannot receive postings — only leaf accounts with `allowPosting = true`.
5. **No posting in a Locked or Closed fiscal period.**
6. **No silent edit** after posting or after Qoyod export. Corrections are explicit Reversal, Adjustment, Credit Note, or Debit Note.
7. **No hard delete** of accounting records. Soft delete + reversal only.
8. **Tenant scope** is mandatory on every accounting record and every query.
9. **Tax category** on every invoice line is captured as a **snapshot** at posting time; later changes to the tax category master do not retroactively change historical invoices.
10. **Customer Advances** are liabilities, never revenue, until applied to a final invoice.
11. **Transformation/Blending** never produces revenue or COGS; it is a cost-preserving inventory move.
12. **Order Allocation** never produces COGS; only shipping/delivery/sale/closure (per policy) produces COGS.

1. Operational service writes the operational record (e.g., RoastingBatch, ShipmentLine, ReceiptVoucher).
2. Operational service emits an Accounting Event (in the same DB transaction) with `event_type`, `source_module`, `source_document_id`, `tenant_id`, `branch_id`, `occurred_at`, and payload.
3. AccountingService translates the event into one or more Draft Journal Entries using the tenant's policies (costing method, COGS policy, waste treatment, VAT category, branch-accounting mode, account mappings).
4. If approval is required (amount limit, document type, role, branch, risk flag), the draft is held in `Submitted` state until approved.
5. On approval (or auto-post for low-risk auto-generated entries), the journal entry is posted: status → `Posted`, period stamp applied, balances updated.
6. If Qoyod export is enabled, an Export Job is enqueued (idempotent by `external_export_key`).
7. Audit log entry is written.

- Posting on a parent account.
- Posting with `debit ≠ credit`.
- Posting outside an open fiscal period.
- Posting without an account mapping for the event type (must fail loudly, not silently default).
- Applying the same customer advance twice.
- Generating COGS at order allocation.
- Treating a blend as new production.
- Editing or deleting a posted/exported entry.
- Hardcoding VAT rate anywhere in code.
- Cross-tenant access to chart of accounts, invoices, or journal entries.

- Purchase invoices above a configurable amount limit.
- Supplier payments above a configurable amount limit.
- Journal entries above a configurable amount limit.
- Reversal of any posted journal entry.
- Manual adjustment entries.
- Period closing.
- Use of non-default tax category (Zero-rated, Exempt, Out of Scope).
- Override of overdue-customer order block.
- Abnormal roasting loss treatment (when outside the normal range).
- Waste records above configured thresholds.
- Customer advance refund.
- Inventory variance posting (after physical count).
- Editing approval rules themselves (must require higher-level role).
- Qoyod export execution and re-export.

1. Barista starts a Calibration Session on a station screen (Branch, Machine, Grinder, Bean, Lot must be selected).
2. Each calibration shot is logged (mandatory per shot — no aggregate-only entries).
3. System computes total dose; if shots > `reviewRequiredShotsThreshold` or waste% > `calibrationWastePercentageThreshold` → approval required.
4. On finish, inventory is deducted from Roasted Coffee Inventory.
5. Accounting Event emitted: **Dr Calibration Waste / Cr Roasted Coffee Inventory**.
6. Calibration Waste record created and linked to the inventory movement and the JE.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━5 Workflow E — QC Testing Waste

1. After a Roasting Batch completes (or as part of QC sampling), a QC Test is started.
2. QC user selects Test Type (Cupping, Espresso Test, Filter Test, Sample Roast, Roast Defect, Complaint Investigation, Retest, Shelf-Life).
3. Bean / Batch / Lot is selected; sample count and dose per sample entered (waste auto-calculated).
4. Approval required above threshold.
5. Inventory deducted from Roasted Coffee or Finished Goods inventory.
6. Accounting Event emitted: **Dr QC Testing Waste / Cr Roasted/FG Inventory**.
7. Batch quality status updated.
8. For Complaint Investigation tests, link to the complaint record is mandatory.

SECTION 6 — WORKFLOW / PROCESS LOGIC
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━6 Workflow F — Roasting Output & Roasting Loss

1. Roasting batch begins (operational module). Green coffee weight in.
2. Roasting completes. Roasted output weight recorded.
3. Roasting Loss = Green Input – Roasted Output. Loss % computed.
4. If loss% within normal range → normal loss is absorbed into roasted cost. JE: **Dr Roasted Coffee WIP / Cr Green Coffee Inventory** (entire green cost transferred).
5. If loss% outside normal range → flagged as **Possible Abnormal Loss**. Reason + approval required. After approval: JE separates normal vs abnormal portion. Abnormal portion: **Dr Abnormal Roasting Loss (expense) / Cr Green Coffee WIP**.
6. Cost per roasted kg recomputed for valuation.

SECTION 6 — WORKFLOW / PROCESS LOGIC
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━7 Workflow G — Product Transformation / Blend

1. Operator selects source batches/lots with quantities (e.g., Batch A 3 kg + Batch B 2 kg).
2. System reserves source quantities.
3. New Blend Batch (Batch C) is created with combined cost = sum of source costs (or + actual processing cost if non-zero).
4. Accounting Event: **Dr New Blend Inventory / Cr Source Batch Inventory** — same total cost, no P&L impact.
5. Tax category for the event = Out of Scope (internal, non-sale).
6. The dashboard counts this as Transformation, **not** production. No revenue, no COGS, no inventory inflation.

SECTION 6 — WORKFLOW / PROCESS LOGIC
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━8 Workflow H — Qoyod Export (Phase 1: CSV/Excel; Phase 2: API)

1. A posted journal entry (or batch of entries) becomes eligible for export when `tenant.export_to_qoyod = true` and Qoyod mapping is complete for all accounts in the entry.
2. Export Job enqueued with a unique `external_export_key` derived from (`tenant_id`, `journal_entry_id`, `version`) — idempotent.
3. Phase 1: a scheduled job exports rows to CSV/Excel; user downloads and uploads to Qoyod; user marks job as Exported with Qoyod reference.
4. Phase 2: API push to Qoyod; response (success + Qoyod ID, or error) stored as `responseSnapshot`.
5. On failure: status = Export Failed, error message stored, retry up to N times with backoff.
6. Reconciliation Report compares ERP postings vs Qoyod reference for a period, flags differences.
7. If a posted entry needs correction after export → Reversal JE + new JE; both exported; original is **never edited**.

1. Period close requested for month M.
2. Pre-close checklist: review invoices, payments, advances, AR/AP aging, inventory valuation, COGS, waste, VAT, Qoyod export/reconciliation.
3. Locked period: no new postings with dates within M; only adjustments via subsequent open period.
4. Closed period: even adjustments require a higher-level override; closing entries are computed (P&L → Retained Earnings).
5. Reports generated and snapshotted.

SECTION 8 — INVENTORY IMPACT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ Inventory Impact

The accounting module is **the primary consumer** of inventory movement events, and many of its rules constrain inventory behavior:

- **Goods Receipt** increases Raw Inventory at cost; the cost basis enters the costing engine (Weighted Average / FIFO / Standard depending on tenant setting).
- **Raw Material Consumption** (e.g., green coffee into roasting) decreases Raw Inventory and increases WIP.
- **Roasting Output** decreases Green/WIP and increases Roasted Coffee Inventory at the rolled cost (incl. normal loss absorption).
- **Packaging Consumption** decreases Packaging Materials inventory and increases Finished Goods cost.
- **Finished Goods Creation** records FG with full rolled cost.
- **Calibration Waste / QC Testing Waste** decrease Roasted/FG Inventory and charge to dedicated waste accounts — **not** to general COGS.
- **Transformation/Blend** moves cost from source to destination lot **without** changing total inventory value (no revenue, no COGS).
- **Allocation/Reservation** does **not** change inventory value; it only marks quantity as committed.
- **Shipping/Delivery/Sale** (per COGS policy) decreases FG Inventory and recognizes COGS.
- **Inventory Variance** from physical count creates an adjustment posting that hits `5700 Inventory Variance / Shrinkage`; never silent.
- **Negative inventory is disallowed by default** (`allowNegativeInventory = false`); allow-list per account type per tenant setting if ever needed.

### Anti-double-count rules

- Transformation must never be counted as new production in dashboards or yield reports.
- Allocation is not COGS.
- Customer advance receipt is not revenue.
- Returned-from-customer inventory increases FG but does not retroactively erase original COGS unless the sale is fully reversed via Credit Note.

SECTION 9 — ACCOUNTING / COSTING IMPACT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ Accounting / Costing Impact

This module **is** the accounting layer; everything in §6, §8, §11, §12, §13, §14, §15 (waste), §16, §17, §18 is accounting impact. Highlights:

- **Inventory valuation method** is tenant-configurable: Weighted Average (default), FIFO, Standard Cost. The method is **fixed once postings exist**; switching methods retroactively is disallowed and requires a controlled migration (out of Phase 1 scope).
- **COGS policy** is tenant-configurable per channel (B2B: OnShipping or OnDelivery; POS: OnSale; Online: OnFulfillment/OnShipping).
- **Overhead allocation** is **disabled in Phase 1** (Direct Cost Only). Phase 2 enables Production Overhead Allocation against Normal Capacity (not against actual low-volume runs, which would distort unit cost).
- **Roasting Loss**: Normal absorbed into roasted cost; Abnormal expensed.
- **Calibration / QC / Training Waste**: separate accounts (5500 / 5600 / 5900).
- **Customer Advances** booked to liability `2410`, not revenue, until applied.
- **VAT** is policy-driven via `TaxCategory` records; the rate is **never hardcoded**; snapshot is captured per invoice line.
- **Branch accounting** defaults to Cost Center; later upgradable to Separate Accounts or Hybrid without data migration if the model carries `branch_id` and `cost_center_id` from day one.
- **Period control**: postings outside the open period are rejected.

| `accounting.reports.view` / `.export` | Reports |
| `accounting.auditLog.view` | Audit visibility |

Hard rules:

- Soft delete only for accounting records; never hard delete posted documents.
- Approval rules themselves require a **higher** role to modify (prevent self-elevation).
- Reversal requires explicit permission and a mandatory reason.
- Period reopen is restricted to a top-level role; every reopen is alerted to all admins.
- `accounting.settings.manage` is **not** the same as `accounting.approvalRules.manage`; they are separate to prevent a single admin from disabling the approval system silently.
- Tenant Owner cannot bypass approval thresholds for sensitive operations (reversal, period close) — even owners post through the workflow; this prevents a single compromised account from undoing history.

- Debit=Credit on every JE.
- Account is leaf and `allowPosting`.
- Period is Open for the transaction date.
- Tax category exists for tenant and is Active.
- Mapping exists for Qoyod export if export enabled.
- Approval thresholds evaluated against `ApprovalRule` set for the tenant.
- Advance can only be applied up to its remaining balance.
- Reversal links to original JE; cannot reverse an already-reversed JE.
- Settings changes that affect historical computations (costing method, COGS policy) are blocked when historical postings exist; require a controlled "policy change" process.

Each of the following must be a single ACID transaction (or saga with compensations if cross-service):

- Posting a JE: write JE + lines + balance update + accounting event linkage + audit log.
- Posting an invoice: write invoice + lines + JE + AR/AP update + audit log.
- Recording a receipt: write receipt + JE + invoice payment-status update + advance update if applicable + audit log.
- Applying an advance: validate available balance + write application + JE + invoice update + audit log.
- Roasting output: inventory move (green→roasted) + cost rollup + JE + audit log.
- Waste records: inventory deduction + waste record + JE + audit log.
- Transformation: source consumption + destination creation + cost copy + JE + audit log.
- Qoyod export attempt: status change + payload/response snapshots + retry counter + audit log.
- Period close: lock flag + closing entries + report snapshots + audit log.

- Storing the chart of accounts in localStorage and trusting it.

- Operational event commits but Accounting Event fails → operation must roll back, or be queued for reliable retry; never leave the system in a state where stock moved but no JE exists.
- Qoyod API down → export job retries; user sees Export Failed; manual fallback (CSV) available.
- Approval rule misconfiguration creates an unapprovable invoice → tenant admin override path (with audit) is required to unblock.
- Two concurrent receipts on the same invoice → row-level lock; partial payments must be serializable.
- Tax category deleted/disabled after invoices exist → blocked at the master service; only deactivation is allowed and only for new invoices.
- Mapping missing during export → export halts for that JE; user gets a clear actionable error; never silent skip.
- Negative balance attempt on advance application → rejected with explicit error.

SECTION 12 — SAAS READINESS REVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ SaaS Readiness Review

- **Tenant scope is end-to-end.** Settings, CoA, tax categories, fiscal periods, mappings, journals, invoices, receipts, payments, expenses, advances, waste records, reports — all carry `tenant_id`. RLS or query filter enforced.
- **Per-tenant chart of accounts**: each tenant gets a template-cloned CoA on tenant creation; tenants may extend/customize but not rename system-defined parent codes.
- **Per-tenant defaults** (per source file §30): costing method, COGS policy, branch mode, credit policy, currency, tax category, waste treatment, export settings. Stored in `AccountingSettings`.
- **Subscription gating**: Phase 2 features (POS integration, Qoyod API, advanced branch accounting, multi-currency, SaaS tenant accounting reports) should be plan-gated via feature flags. ZATCA invoice emission may also be plan-gated.
- **Cross-tenant leak risks**:
  - Account ID collisions across tenants → mitigated by `(tenant_id, code)` unique constraints, never `code` global.
  - Approval rules from one tenant accidentally applied to another → rule lookup must be tenant-scoped.
  - Qoyod mapping mixing → mapping table is tenant-scoped; Qoyod credentials are tenant-scoped.
  - Reports must filter by tenant; "global" admin reports require a platform role and explicit cross-tenant header.
- **Per-tenant numbering sequences**: invoice numbers, JE numbers, advance numbers — never global. Format configurable per tenant within ZATCA constraints.
- **Multi-tenant Qoyod credentials**: credentials encrypted at rest; per tenant; never logged in payload snapshots in plaintext.
- **Tenant configuration required**:
  - Fiscal year start
  - Costing method
  - COGS policies per channel
  - Tax categories + default
  - Branch list and branch accounting mode
  - Approval rules
  - Customer credit policy
  - Qoyod mapping + credentials (if used)
  - Account map per accounting event type (e.g., what account is "Roasted Coffee Inventory" for this tenant)
- **Tenant migration / cloning**: when a new tenant is created, system templates the CoA, tax categories, and approval rule starter set. Never copy data from another tenant.
- **Tenant onboarding for accounting**: explicit "Accounting Setup Wizard" must be passed before the tenant can post anything. This prevents tenants from posting with missing mappings.

- Mappings: ERP Account ↔ Qoyod Account, ERP VAT ↔ Qoyod Tax, ERP Customer ↔ Qoyod Customer, ERP Supplier ↔ Qoyod Supplier, ERP Product ↔ Qoyod Product, ERP Payment Method ↔ Qoyod Bank/Cash, ERP Branch/Cost Center ↔ Qoyod Cost Center.
- Idempotency: `external_export_key` is `{tenant_id, journal_entry_id, version}`. Same key must not produce two Qoyod records.
- Failure handling: explicit Export Failed state, retry with backoff, manual re-export by permission.
- Reconciliation: Qoyod Reconciliation Report compares ERP postings vs Qoyod-side records for a period; flagged differences require explanation.
- Decommissioning Qoyod: when ERP becomes the official invoice source, Qoyod export transitions from "required" to "optional" by feature flag without breaking historical data.

SECTION 17 — EDGE CASES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ Edge Cases

- A customer pays more than the invoice → Overpayment status; system creates a customer credit (or refund) record, never a silent rounding.
- A customer pays less than the invoice → Partially Paid; aging clock continues.
- Customer advance refund after partial application → only the unapplied portion can be refunded; applied portion can only be reversed by reversing the original invoice/credit note.
- Same supplier invoice received twice → unique constraint on `(tenant_id, supplier_id, supplier_invoice_number, invoice_date)` rejects duplicates.
- Goods Receipt without Purchase Invoice → accrued liability `GR/IR` (Goods Received / Invoice Received) account; invoice later clears the accrual.
- Purchase Invoice arrives before Goods Receipt → allowed for service invoices; for inventory, hold for matching.
- Roasting Loss < 0 (output > input) → physically impossible; reject and flag for investigation.
- Calibration session with 0 shots → cannot record waste (system should reject empty session).
- QC test for a batch that has been fully shipped → must allow (complaint investigation) but waste is deducted from a separately tracked retained-sample account, not FG.
- Transformation that increases total weight (e.g., absorbing moisture) → flagged as anomaly; explicit override required; cost is not increased.
- POS sale arrives backdated to a Locked period → blocked; queued for adjustment in the open period.
- Tax category changed at master → historical invoices retain snapshot; new invoices use new rate.
- Customer becomes overdue mid-order: if policy = AlertOnly, order proceeds with alert; if BlockOrders, order requires approval to continue.
- Approval rule deleted while a request is pending → request is invalidated and reassigned per fallback rule; never auto-approved silently.
- Multi-currency invoice in Phase 1: the source explicitly says SAR-only in Phase 1, but the schema carries currency fields. The service must reject non-SAR invoices until multi-currency is enabled, never store with implicit currency.
- Reversal of a JE that has been exported to Qoyod: ERP posts Reversal JE; Qoyod export of the reversal is required; the original Qoyod entry is **not** mutated.
- Period close with pending approvals → block close; show outstanding items.
- Negative stock attempt via waste record → reject (default policy).
- POS daily summary differs from imported sales → reconciliation report flags; manual adjustment with reason.
- Same operational event causes accounting event twice due to retry → idempotency key on `source_document_id + event_type + version` rejects the second.
- User changes inventory costing method while history exists → blocked; requires a controlled migration outside Phase 1.

SECTION 18 — QUESTIONS / AMBIGUITIES / RISKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━2 Risks (classified)

| ID | Risk | Class | Recommended fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Operational event commits without Accounting Event due to non-transactional emit | **Critical** | Outbox pattern: write the operational record and the accounting event in the same DB transaction; a separate worker dispatches the event. Eliminates inconsistency. |
| R2 | Qoyod export keys not idempotent → duplicate entries in Qoyod | **Critical** | Enforce unique `external_export_key`; both sides (ERP queue dedup + Qoyod reference check before retry). |
| R3 | Silent edit after posting (e.g., dev shortcut admin tool, direct DB update by support engineer) | **Critical** | Database triggers reject UPDATE on posted JE lines; only Reversal-JE creation is permitted; audited. |
| R4 | Costing method change mid-history corrupts valuation | **High** | Block change when postings exist; require a documented migration plan + period close + reopen with audit. |
| R5 | Tax rate hardcoded in any service or report | **High** | Code review rule + automated test: search for `15`, `0.15`, "VAT" literals; all rate uses must go through `TaxCategoryService`. |
| R6 | Approval rule misconfigured → no one can approve → operations blocked | **High** | Fallback rule (e.g., Tenant Owner always last-resort) + admin "stuck approvals" dashboard. |
| R7 | Per-tenant CoA collision when codes mismatch with Qoyod mapping | **High** | Mapping completeness check before enabling export; visible "missing mapping" alert. |
| R8 | Branch accounting upgrade (CostCenter → SeparateAccounts) breaks historical reports | **High** | Carry `branch_id` and `cost_center_id` on every line from day one; upgrade is a settings flip that affects only new postings unless the tenant requests retroactive split (controlled migration). |
| R9 | Customer Advance applied twice due to race condition | **High** | Row-level lock on the advance; check `remaining_amount` inside transaction; idempotency on application creation. |
| R10 | POS sale duplicated on retry import | **High** | `(tenant_id, pos_provider, external_id)` unique constraint; dedup before posting. |
| R11 | Reversal of an exported JE creates Qoyod desync | **High** | Reversal must also export; explicit reconciliation flag for reversed entries; do not edit original. |
| R12 | Period reopen allowed too easily | **Medium** | Highest role only; mandatory reason; alert to all admins; audit. |
| R13 | Calibration / QC waste used to "hide" abnormal roasting loss | **Medium** | Hard separation in code; periodic anomaly report compares waste category trends vs production volume. |
| R14 | Transformation/Blend used to inflate inventory by entering more output than input | **Medium** | Enforce output qty ≤ input qty (within tolerance); excess requires override with reason; never auto-creates revenue. |
| R15 | Customer advance treated as revenue in dashboards | **Medium** | Dashboard derives revenue strictly from `Revenue` accounts (4xxx); advances live in `2410` and are not summed into revenue. |
| R16 | Multi-tenant query missing tenant filter | **Critical** | Row-Level Security in DB; every query goes through tenant-scoped repository; static analysis check. |
| R17 | Approval thresholds editable by the same user who needs to be approved | **Critical** | Separation: `accounting.approvalRules.manage` requires a strictly higher role than the approval being modified; tested invariant. |
| R18 | ZATCA fields not populated during transition → migration to ERP-issued invoices becomes painful | **High** | Capture ZATCA fields from Qoyod responses and store on ERP invoices today, even if Qoyod is the issuer. |
| R19 | Period-close report snapshots not stored → past reports become unreproducible after later corrections | **High** | Snapshot reports at close time; later "corrected" reports are separate documents. |
| R20 | Qoyod credentials stored in plaintext or logged in payload snapshots | **Critical** | Encrypt credentials; redact sensitive fields from payload/response snapshots; key rotation policy. |
| R21 | Negative inventory allowed by misconfiguration → COGS computed against nothing | **High** | Default `allowNegativeInventory = false`; enabling requires platform-admin permission + per-account scope. |
| R22 | Customer credit policy AlertOnly silently turned off, allowing huge overdue exposure | **Medium** | Settings change is audited; weekly digest to Tenant Owner about overdue exposure. |
| R23 | Approval log appended to but readable cross-tenant | **Critical** | AuditLog and ApprovalLog tenant-scoped; tested invariant. |
| R24 | Reports computed from caches that diverge from ledger | **Medium** | Nightly reconciliation; cache invalidation on every JE post; reports include `as_of` timestamp. |
| R25 | A B2C customer's accumulated café POS sales are not aggregated into a customer account for CRM | **Low** | Decide whether POS is anonymous or customer-linked. If linked, ensure AR sub-ledger handles many small entries efficiently. |
| R26 | Sales Rep commission accruals not modeled | **Medium** | Add explicit accrual logic in Phase 2; otherwise commissions ride below the line and distort margin. |
| R27 | Inventory revaluation when costing method or cost layers shift causes mass JE posting that may cross period boundaries | **High** | Revaluations always post on a single dated entry within the current open period; older periods are never touched. |
